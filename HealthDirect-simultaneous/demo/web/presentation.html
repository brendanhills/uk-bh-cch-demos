🔊 Google Meet Audio Sharing Guide

To ensure both original and translated audio from both channels share natively in your Google Meet presentation, follow these simple steps:

  1. Inside Google Meet, click the Present Now (screen icon) button in the bottom-middle controls bar.
  2. Select the "Chrome Tab" (or "A Tab") sharing option.
    Important: Avoid sharing your "Entire Screen" or "Window" as browsers do not allow system audio sharing through those methods.
  3. Select this "HealthDirect Presentation Console" tab from the list of tabs.
  4. At the bottom-left corner of the popup dialog, ensure the checkbox labeled "Also share tab audio" is checked!
✅ This directs Chrome to stream both channels natively into Google Meet as a single digital feed, guaranteeing perfect stereo output and speaker isolation.
