The security complexity: This is a bank—access control is not optional, it's legally mandated:
●​ Chinese wall: Investment banking employees cannot see trading floor communications about certain
clients
●​ HR confidentiality: Only HR and direct managers can see performance reviews
●​ Regulatory: Compliance documents have specific access lists; audit logs must show who accessed
what
●​ Geographic: EU employees' data must comply with GDPR; some data cannot be processed in US
datacenters
Specific access control scenarios to handle:
1.​ A trader searches 'client gift policy'—they should see the trading-specific policy, not the investment
banking version

careers.google.com

2.​ An HR manager searches 'termination process for John Smith'—they should see relevant HR docs only
for their direct reports
3.​ A compliance officer searches 'trading violations'—they should see audit logs, but not HR files about the
traders involved
4.​ An IT support tech searches 'password reset'—they should see ServiceNow knowledge articles, not
Workday admin procedures
The customer wants Gemini Enterprise / Agentspace that:
1.​ Provides unified natural language search across all 5 systems
2.​ Respects existing access controls—users see only what they're authorized to see in source systems
3.​ Uses existing Okta SSO—no separate login or credential management
4.​ Maintains complete audit trail of all searches and document access
5.​ Keeps data in the bank's environment—no data sent to public APIs
Technical challenges you must address:
1.​ Authentication flow: User authenticates via Okta → How does that identity propagate to ServiceNow,
Workday, and the legacy compliance system?
2.​ ACL synchronization: Source systems have their own permission models—how do you translate these
for search?
3.​ Connector architecture: No first-party connectors exist—you must build custom connectors for each
system
4.​ Token management: How do you handle token exchange across different identity providers without
storing long-lived credentials?
5.​ Legacy system: The compliance system uses LDAP+2FA with no OAuth—how do you integrate it
securely?
Questions to address:
●​ Walk me through the complete authentication flow when a user performs a search
●​ How do you implement Workload Identity Federation to avoid storing credentials?
●​ How do you handle the Okta → Azure AD → ServiceNow token delegation chain?
●​ What happens if a document's permissions change after it's been indexed?
●​ How do you design audit logging that satisfies regulators?
Design the connector architecture and authentication flow. This is primarily a security and identity
federation challenge—the search quality is secondary to getting access control right.

Your Audience
You, the domain specialist, have been engaged to build that architecture and present it to the customer’s domain
expert. You are presenting the validated proof-of-concept and final technical design to the customer’s domain
expert (Technical Stakeholder) and their VP of Strategy (Business Stakeholder). Your interviewer will ask
questions to represent both perspectives.

Presentation Logistics
●​ Important: Be sure not to include confidential information in your presentation; including the name of
the customer example being used. Avoid using logos, screenshots or imagery that could breach
confidentiality. Please make the best decision based on your current workplace agreements.
●​ While our goal is to evaluate your ability to step into a Google Cloud CE role, this interview is not meant
to be an exhaustive demonstration of your mastery of Google's product features or naming conventions.
Please feel free to leverage your experience with other public cloud providers and products in the
market as part of your presentation.
●​ You’re welcome to use any presentation software (including but not limited to: Google Slides, PPT,
Adobe, Prezi, Keynote). Please note that certain software may take over your entire presentation
window. Be sure to practice presenting via Google Meet before your interview.
●​ Practice your presentation without your notes. You may use notes during your presentation, but know
that if you share your screen, the interviewers may be able to see them.

Best Practices
●​ Build your presentation with simplicity and intention. The panel will assess verbal communication and
presentation content and structure.
●​ We want this to be fun, not burdensome! The presentation should take no more than approximately four
hours to prepare.
●​ Anticipate and prepare for questions you might be asked during and after you finish.
●​ Practice! Your delivery should be conversational but professional. Let your Recruiter know if you have any
questions about your presentation format.
