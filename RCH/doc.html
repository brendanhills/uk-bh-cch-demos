RE: Occupational Therapy Mobility Assessment - John Silva

Dear Care Manager,

I am writing to provide an update following the comprehensive home-based occupational therapy mobility assessment conducted for Mr. John Silva at his residence. The assessment was requested to review his current level of functional mobility, evaluate safety risks within his immediate living environment, and optimize his overall independence.

Mr. Silva currently resides alone in his home. Recently, he has experienced a self-reported decline in his overall lower-limb strength and balance, leading to heightened instability and an increased fear of falling during basic activities of daily living (ADLs). During the assessment, his gait was observed using his existing single-point walking cane. This assessment revealed that the cane no longer provides adequate base support or lateral stability, placing him at a high risk of falls, particularly on uneven surfaces or during transfers.

To directly address these safety concerns, promote ongoing functional independence, and support his desire to age safely in place, the following targeted home equipment and modifications are highly recommended:

  • 1. Four-wheeled Walker: A standard 4-wheel walker (rollator) equipped with pressure-activated hand brakes, a built-in padded seat, and an under-seat basket is strongly recommended. This device will broaden his base of support, significantly improve gait symmetry and stability, reduce fatigue, and provide a secure, immediate seating option during periods of exertion both indoors and outdoors.
  • 2. Front Entrance Access Ramp: The supply and installation of a modular, slip-resistant access ramp at the primary front threshold of the home is recommended. This modification will eliminate the existing vertical step barrier, preventing potential tripping hazards and facilitating safe, uninterrupted transition of the four-wheeled walker during community entry and exit.

Please feel free to contact me directly on the numbers provided above should you require any additional information or further clarification regarding these clinical recommendations.
