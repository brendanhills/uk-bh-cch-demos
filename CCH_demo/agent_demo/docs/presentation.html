Framing The Problem
The Caregiver's Burden

The Anxiety of Hospital-to-Home Pediatric Recovery

01

Complex Discharge Overload

Parents are handed complex, jargon-heavy instructions and medication dosing schedules for respiratory illnesses like Asthma/RSV during periods of peak emotional stress.

02

Fragmented Clinical Scheduling

Securing post-discharge home care support or clinical nurse visits is historically administrative, disconnected, and slow—leaving families feeling abandoned in the crucial first 48 hours.

03

Opaque Cost & Subsidy Barriers

Parents struggle to calculate home care fees, navigate complex NDIS or Medicare rules, and apply funding eligibility while focused purely on their child's recovery.

04

The Home Information Black Hole

Valuable clinical insights, vital sign readings, and nursing actions taken at home are rarely integrated back into the hospital's Electronic Medical Record (EMR).
