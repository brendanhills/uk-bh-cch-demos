# Product Guidelines: RCH Resource & Staff Co-Scheduling x AlphaEvolve

## Visual Identity & Branding
To build immediate trust and familiarity for hospital staff, the dashboard will adhere to the following visual guidelines:
- **Color Scheme:** Primary use of Cymbal Children's Hospital (CCH) official colors (Professional Green, Blue, and White).
- **Style:** Clean, uncluttered, and professional, ensuring that data and outcomes are the primary focus.

## UI/UX Principles
The user interface is explicitly designed to deliver a "Wow" factor for non-technical stakeholders, prioritizing clear visualization of complex algorithmic improvements:
- **Primary Layout:** An Interactive Schedule Grid / Gantt Chart. This must feature smooth animations showing shift blocks and resource assignments shifting and resolving conflicts as AlphaEvolve optimizes in real-time.
- **The Comparison Toggle:** A prominent "Baseline vs. AlphaEvolve Plan" toggle (inspired by the original Kmart demo). This allows stakeholders to instantly see the dramatic improvements the algorithm makes over the naive or manual schedule.
- **Real-Time Metrics Dashboard:** A clear side-panel or header displaying live counters ticking up (e.g., Patient Throughput, Resource Utilization) and down (e.g., Staff Overtime Hours) as the evolutionary process runs.
- **Simplicity Focus:** To keep the demo focused and accessible for non-technical audiences, technical details (like algorithm code diffs) and complex visual alerts (like constraint violation flags) will be omitted to keep the interface clean and impactful.

## Tone & Voice
All UI labels, tooltips, and status messages must be:
- **Professional & Clear:** Respectful of the hospital environment.
- **Action-Oriented:** Focused on outcomes and benefits (e.g., "Optimize Schedule", "Throughput Improved").
- **Jargon-Free:** Avoiding technical AI or evolutionary terms (e.g., preferring "Improving schedule..." over "Running generation 45 of the genetic algorithm").
