---
description: Process for capturing 'ALWAYS' and 'NEVER' rules from user feedback.
---

1.  **Monitor**: Listen for phrases like "ALWAYS do X", "NEVER do Y", "Make sure to always...", or "Don't ever...".
2.  **Verify**: Confirm the rule is project-wide and not just context-specific.
3.  **Update**:
    -   Open `.agents/project_rules.md`.
    -   Add the rule under the appropriate section ("ALWAYS Rules" or "NEVER Rules").
    -   Keep the rule concise and actionable.
4.  **Confirm**: Reply to the user: "I have added '[Rule]' to the project rules."
5.  **Enforce**: Check `.agents/project_rules.md` periodically or before major tasks to ensure compliance.
