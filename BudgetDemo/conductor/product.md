# Product Guide: Australian Budget Analysis Dashboard & Data Extraction Tool

## 1. Product Overview
This tool is a Python-based budget analysis dashboard and data extraction system built using Streamlit. It is designed to help financial analysts, policy researchers, and decision-makers easily query, analyze, and compare Australian budget data, GDP growth indicators, and nominal GDP from official source files (such as Budget Paper No. 1 and associated spreadsheets/CSVs).

## 2. Target Audience
- **Financial Analysts & Policy Researchers:** Professionals who require deep, precise budget data extraction, trend analysis, and comparisons between different budget papers and economic indicators.
- **Economic Analysts:** Users focused on examining Australian GDP growth, nominal GDP, and their relation to budget projections.

## 3. Core Objectives
- **Data Democratization & Analysis:** Simplify the process of viewing, plotting, and comparing complex Australian budget papers and tables.
- **AI-Powered Retrieval:** Allow users to ask natural language questions about budget documents and receive verified answers cited directly from source PDFs.
- **Seamless Integration:** Provide unified access to nominal GDP Excel spreadsheets, CSV data, and PDF reports.

## 4. Key Features (Phase 1)
- **Interactive Budget Dashboard:**
  - High-fidelity visualization of Australian GDP growth indicators and nominal GDP.
  - Interactive tables and charts driven by local CSV and Excel files.
- **AI Chat Assistant (PDF Q&A):**
  - Semantic search and Q&A over the official Australian Budget Paper 1 (`bp1_2026-27.pdf` and related chapters `bp1_bs-*.pdf`).
  - Answer citation matching, displaying corresponding PDF pages or sections.
- **Data Export & Reports:**
  - Ability to download clean, filtered tables as CSV.
  - Summarize key findings or policy highlights.

## 5. Scope of Data
- **Primary Source Data:** Official Australian budget documents (specifically Budget Paper 1 for 2026-27 and historical tables/spreadsheets provided locally).
- **Format Support:** PDF documents, Excel spreadsheets (.xlsx), and CSV files.
